## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for a focused, contemplative literary reading and quote curation experience. Rooted in the tactile resonance of printed volumes and modern digital editorial curation, the aesthetic bridges timeless book publishing with the fluid, systemic adaptability of Material 3.

The interface evokes quiet contemplation, scholarly delight, and focused curation. Visual friction is deliberately minimized; typography takes center stage, supported by ample atmospheric whitespace, calm paper-like backdrops, and deeply saturated accent tones reminiscent of archival ink and rich amber resin. The layout embraces structural calm, warm tactile surfaces, and subtle, intentional state changes over gratuitous ornament.

## Colors

The color system implements a dual-mode Material 3 palette tuned for long-form reading, low glare, and high typographic legibility.

## Typography

The type system creates an editorial conversation between classical literary craft and modern interface clarity.

- **Primary Editorial Serif (Headings & Literary Quotations):** `Newsreader` delivers the literary weight, traditional bookish warmth, and proportions required for long-form quotes and prominent titles.
- **Support & Functional Sans-Serif (Body, Controls, Metas):** `Manrope` handles functional navigation, interactive controls, tags, metadata, and body notes, maintaining geometric legibility without distracting from the literature.

### Stylistic Directives
- **Quote Styling:** All quote text rendered in `quote-featured` and `quote-body` styles must use `font-style: italic`.
- **Author Attribution & Citations:** Set using `label-lg` or `label-md` in `Manrope` with an em-dash prefix (`— Author Name, Source`).
- **Punctuation & Ligatures:** Enable standard typographic ligatures and directional quotation marks (“ ”) for all rendered quotes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptable, fluid column grid designed primarily for mobile-first handheld reading, scaling cleanly to tablets and desktop study views.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Compact (Mobile: < 600px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Single-stream flow focusing on one reading card at a time with generous vertical air.
- **Medium (Tablet: 600px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Accommodates dual-pane reading, split between active quote detail and curated collections.
- **Expanded (Desktop: ≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a max-width container of `1200px` centered with auto margins; content blocks span 6 to 8 columns for optimal reading measure (45–75 characters per line).

### Spacing Rhythm
Consistent spacing multiples of 4px/8px drive vertical pace. Quote cards maintain generous internal padding (`space-xl` / 32px on compact, `space-2xl` / 48px on expanded displays) to create a framing mat reminiscent of wide book margins.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with Material 3 principles, visual hierarchy is created through **tonal layering** and surface tints rather than sharp drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `surface` / `background` (`#FBF8F3` light / `#13131A` dark).
2. **Level 1 (Default Cards & Surfaces):** `surface-container` (`#F4EFEA` light / `#22222A` dark). Flat, resting directly on canvas without drop shadows.
3. **Level 2 (Active / Lifted Cards):** `surface-container-high` (`#EFE9E3`) combined with an ambient, diffused shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(61, 74, 140, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Bottom Sheets, Popovers):** Highest surface-container elevation paired with:
   `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(28, 27, 31, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 27, 31, 0.04)`.

### Border Outlines
When cards require deliberate structural separation, use a low-contrast border (`1px solid var(--outline-variant)`) at 20% opacity rather than crisp borders.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes softness, comfortable hand-held ergonomics, and calm visual containment.

- **Primary Interactive Elements (Buttons, Segmented Controls, Search Bars, Badges):** Full pill geometry (`roundedness: 3`, `border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Quote Cards & Surface Containers:** Large, continuous corner curvature with explicit radii between `24px` (`rounded-lg`) and `32px` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Small Contextual Tags & Floating Badges:** Circular or 9999px fully rounded caps.
- **Text Input Fields & Dropdowns:** `16px` to `24px` radius to reflect card softness while preserving compact vertical form.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Filled Button (Primary Action):** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), height `48px`, background `primary` (`#3D4A8C`), text `on-primary` (`#FFFFFF`), `label-lg` in `Manrope`. No shadow.
- **Tonal Button (Secondary Action):** Pill-shaped, background `secondary-container` (`#FFDEA6`), text `on-secondary-container` (`#271900`).
- **Outlined / Text Button:** Pill-shaped, transparent background with `1px` outline in `outline` (`#787680`), padding `12px 24px`.

### Segmented Button Controls
- Enclosed pill-shaped outer track in `surface-container` (`#F4EFEA`) with `4px` internal padding.
- Active segment: Pill background in `primary-container` (`#DDE1FF`) with label in `on-primary-container` (`#001453`), font `label-md`.
- Inactive segment: Transparent background, text `on-surface-variant` (`#47464F`).

### Quote Cards
- Surface background set to `surface-container` (`#F4EFEA`) with `border-radius: 28px` and internal padding of `28px` to `32px`.
- Quote body rendered in `Newsreader` italic, 600 weight, sized via `quote-featured` or `quote-body`.
- Attribution row aligned to bottom: horizontal flex container with author avatar or circular icon badge, author name and book title in `label-lg` / `body-sm`, and right-aligned action buttons (save, copy, share).

### Circular Icon Badges
- Floating action indicators, author portraits, and category representations are housed in soft, tinted circular badges (`border-radius: 50%`, size `40px` or `48px`).
- Tint backgrounds draw systematically from container roles:
  - Default: `primary-container` with flat glyph in `primary`.
  - Saved / Favorited: `secondary-container` with flat glyph in `secondary`.
  - Thematic Topics: `tertiary-container` with flat glyph in `tertiary`.

### Chips & Topic Tags
- Compact pill elements (`height: 32px`, `border-radius: 9999px`, padding `0 12px`).
- Unselected: `surface-container-high` background, `body-sm` text in `on-surface-variant`.
- Selected: `tertiary-container` (`#FFD9E2`) background, text in `on-tertiary-container` (`#3B081D`).

### Input Fields
- Outlined container style with `border-radius: 16px`, background in `surface-container`, text in `on-surface`.
- Default border: `1px solid transparent`; Active focus border: `2px solid var(--primary)` with no drop shadow.
- Placeholder text in `on-surface-variant` with `body-md` typography.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular radio selectors and soft-square checkboxes (`rounded-sm`, 4px radius).
- Unchecked: `2px solid var(--outline)` with transparent fill.
- Checked: `primary` fill with white checkmark glyph.
